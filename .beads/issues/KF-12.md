## Problem
escape() only handles \\ \" \n \r \t. Per RFC 8259 and NIP-01, all control chars U+0000-U+001F must be escaped. Missing \b \f and \uXXXX for other control chars.

## Affected Files
- Sources/KeyfobCore/CanonicalJSON.swift

## Fix Strategy
Add escaping for \b, \f, and \uXXXX encoding for all control characters below U+0020.
