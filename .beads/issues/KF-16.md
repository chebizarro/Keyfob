## Problem
generateIfNeeded() sets both kSecAttrAccessible and kSecAttrAccessControl (Apple says not to mix them). Also sets kSecAttrSynchronizable with ThisDeviceOnly accessibility, which contradicts.

## Evidence
KeyManager.swift lines 32-44: conflicting attrs dict.

## Affected Files
- Sources/KeyfobCrypto/KeyManager.swift

## Fix Strategy
Remove kSecAttrAccessible when using kSecAttrAccessControl. Gate synchronizable on non-biometric policy.
